EvalBot

Upload a recorded job interview (English). EvalBot transcribes it, tells the interviewer and candidate apart, analyzes the candidate's voice, and generates a full PDF evaluation report. Processing takes about 1–3 minutes.

WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, WEBM · up to 100 MB
Analyzing…
    Pipeline: AssemblyAI transcription & diarization · NVIDIA NeMo TitaNet speaker embeddings + Pinecone · Random Forest role classifier (trained on the MIT Interview dataset) · librosa voice analysis · Gemini evaluation · ReportLab PDF.
    API: POST /process-audio (form fields user_id + file or file_url). Part of the EvalBot graduation project, Benha University.
